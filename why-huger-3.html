03 · Milestones

Three decades.
One direction.

1988
Foundation
Founded in Guangrao, Dongying — as a waterproof-materials factory.
1995
Into Tyres
Entered the tyre industry — the first tyre enterprise in the region, making agricultural tyres.
1997
Light-Duty Bias
Launched production of light-duty bias (cross-ply) truck tyres.
1999
Medium-Duty Bias
Added medium-duty bias truck-tyre production.
2001
Engineering Tyres
Commissioned an OTR / engineering-tyre production line.
2002
First TBR Plant
Co-built an all-steel radial (TBR) truck & bus tyre plant.
2010
Precision Molds
Began a precision aluminium-magnesium alloy radial-tyre mold facility (1,200 mold-sets/yr).
2011
Intelligent Plant
Began a new intelligent TBR plant rated at 2.8 million tyres per year.
2020
Full-Lifecycle Upgrade
Began upgrading energy, smart-management and production systems across their full lifecycle.
