05 · The Compound

It starts with
the chemistry.

A tyre that lasts isn't built — it's mixed. Our chemistry team formulates compound recipes for every application: low rolling resistance for highway, tear-resistant blocks for mining, cold-flex for European winters.

01
Low-rolling-resistance series

Silica-loaded tread compounds engineered for fuel-economy fleets. Pairs with our FRIDERIC long-haul casings.

02
Cut & chip resistant series

Reinforced shoulder compounds for mining and construction. Multi-block patterns with stone-ejector geometry.

03
Retreadable casings

Casing designs optimized for 2–3 retreads — independently tested under European labelling law.

06 · Manufacturing

Seven processes.
Zero shortcuts.

From rubber mixing to the final X-ray scan — every tyre passes through a fully-instrumented production line and an in-house laboratory. Hover any tile.

Stage 01 · Mixing
Banbury Compound Mixing

Computer-controlled batching ensures every block of compound has identical filler dispersion, density and Mooney viscosity.

Stage 02
Calendering

Four-roll precision sheeting for ply and inner-liner consistency.

Stage 03
Building

Stage 04 · Cure
