02 · The Origin

From Guangrao to the world's roads.

When Shandong Hugerubber was founded in 1988, Guangrao was already known as the "Tire Capital of China". Four decades later we still build every tyre here — in one of the most concentrated rubber-industry ecosystems on the planet. Three management generations have come through the same factory district. The line we run today isn't the line we ran in 2010 — capex never stopped — but the people walking it have, in many cases, been here twenty years.

That continuity matters. It's why our casings hold up to two and three retreads, why our compound recipes evolve quietly between batches rather than swinging on consultant trends, and why a fleet manager in Lagos can call our Hangzhou office and reach someone who personally signed off on the spec sheet.

"We build tyres the way Guangrao has built tyres for forty years. Same district, same chemistry roots, slightly better instruments each season." HUGER Engineering Team

Total assets of ¥1.8 billion sound like a number, but it shows up in concrete places: a Banbury mixer commissioned in 2019, an X-ray non-destructive testing rig that scans every single casing, a steel-cord laboratory that rejects reels other plants would accept. None of that is visible from the road — but it's why 70+ national regulators trust our stamps and 5 continents of fleets keep re-ordering.

05 · The Anatomy

It starts with
the chemistry.

A tyre that lasts isn't built — it's mixed. Every layer is engineered.

01
Tread Compound

Silica-loaded for low rolling resistance · cut & chip resistant blocks for mining.

02
Belt Edge Cushion

Manages strain at belt edges — extends casing fatigue life by 18–25%.

03
Steel Belt Package

4-belt construction with stone-ejector geometry. Tensile-tested every reel.

04
Body Ply (Carcass)

High-tensile steel cord — maintains inflation pressure and resists road shock.

05
Inner Liner

Butyl-rich compound · industry-best air retention, prevents internal oxidation.

06
Bead Bundle

Hexagonal high-tensile steel wires + nylon chafer · zero-leak seal against rim.
